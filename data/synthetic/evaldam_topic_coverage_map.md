# Evaldam Manual Training Coverage Map

Use this as the manual checklist for generating one high-quality JSONL conversation at a time. Each scenario should combine:

- A company stage
- A financing or compliance decision
- A sector context
- Indian legal, tax, scheme, or market reality
- A founder-style question pattern

Avoid baking live dashboard counts or short-lived market statistics into the answer. Use live/current data only to verify official rules, thresholds, scheme names, and recent legal changes before writing a record.

## Startup Stages

1. Idea stage
   - Founder has concept, no MVP, no revenue
   - Questions: Should I incorporate now? Should I raise pre-seed? How much equity for an idea-stage co-founder? Which grant fits before MVP?
   - Keywords: idea validation, founder split, incorporation, pre-seed, MVP budget, NIDHI-PRAYAS, prototype grant

2. Prototype / MVP stage
   - MVP built, early pilots, no meaningful revenue
   - Questions: Grant vs angel money? Free pilots vs paid pilots? IP assignment? Prototype valuation?
   - Keywords: MVP, paid pilot, proof of concept, prototype validation, IP assignment, product-market proof

3. Pilot / early traction
   - Few customers, low MRR, pilot revenue
   - Questions: Which valuation method? Should pilot revenue count? How to price angel round? Is DCF relevant?
   - Keywords: Berkus, Scorecard, ARR multiple, customer concentration, pilot conversion, runway

4. Pre-seed
   - First external money from angels, accelerator, friends and family
   - Questions: SAFE vs CCPS? Convertible note? Angel syndicate terms? Founder dilution?
   - Keywords: CCPS, CCD, convertible note, FEMA, angel round, valuation cap, discount, side letters

5. Seed
   - Institutional seed, INR 1Cr-10Cr type rounds
   - Questions: ESOP pool pre-money or post-money? Liquidation preference? Anti-dilution? Board rights?
   - Keywords: ESOP pool, 1x non-participating liquidation, broad-based weighted average, full ratchet, reserved matters

6. Bridge round
   - Running out of runway before a stronger priced round
   - Questions: Bridge note vs priced seed? Valuation cap fairness? Maturity risk? Existing investor signal?
   - Keywords: bridge note, qualified financing, maturity, interest, MFN, pro-rata, runway extension

7. Series A
   - Repeatable revenue and institutional lead investor
   - Questions: Which lead term sheet? Strategic vs financial VC? ESOP refresh? Board control?
   - Keywords: Series A, lead investor, no-shop, super pro-rata, board seat, reserved matters, investor signaling

8. Series B / growth
   - Scaling revenue, multiple funds, possible secondary
   - Questions: Founder secondary sale? Growth dilution? Down-round protection? Expansion debt?
   - Keywords: Series B, founder liquidity, secondary sale, growth equity, venture debt, revenue quality

9. Pre-IPO / IPO readiness
   - Governance, audit, profitability, public-market path
   - Questions: SME IPO vs mainboard IPO? PE buyout vs strategic sale? Related-party cleanup?
   - Keywords: SME IPO, mainboard IPO, DRHP, SEBI ICDR, corporate governance, independent directors, EBITDA, audit readiness

10. Exit
   - Strategic sale, acquihire, PE buyout, founder rollover
   - Questions: Cash vs earnout? Indemnity cap? Escrow? Tax on share sale? Asset sale vs share sale?
   - Keywords: M&A, earnout, escrow, indemnity, non-compete, slump sale, share purchase agreement, capital gains

## Startup Financing Instruments

1. Equity
   - Ordinary equity, CCPS, preference shares
   - Keywords: CCPS, valuation report, FC-GPR, private placement, rights issue, SHA, AoA

2. Convertible instruments
   - Convertible note, CCD, CCPS conversion, foreign investor constraints
   - Keywords: valuation cap, discount, maturity, qualified financing, FEMA, pricing guidelines

3. SAFE-style requests
   - Usually not clean for Indian Pvt Ltd companies
   - Keywords: YC SAFE, Indian Companies Act, CCPS alternative, Delaware flip, externalization

4. Venture debt
   - Growth-stage debt alongside VC backing
   - Keywords: warrants, interest rate, repayment schedule, covenants, cash runway, non-dilutive capital

5. Revenue-based financing
   - Consumer/D2C/SaaS repayment from revenue
   - Keywords: RBF, revenue share, repayment cap, CAC, gross margin, cash drag

6. Grants and soft capital
   - Non-dilutive or low-dilution public/accelerator support
   - Keywords: SISFS, NIDHI-PRAYAS, TANSEED, RKVY-RAFTAAR, BIRAC BIG, MeitY, state startup policy

7. Strategic capital
   - CVC, corporate partnerships, customer-funded development
   - Keywords: CVC, strategic investor, exclusivity, ROFR, MFN, channel conflict, pilot-to-contract

8. Personal/founder capital
   - Founder loans, sweat equity, reimbursement, founder salary
   - Keywords: founder loan, director remuneration, sweat equity, related-party transaction, tax treatment

## MSME Finance And Credit

1. Udyam registration and classification
   - Micro, Small, Medium thresholds; investment and turnover test
   - Keywords: Udyam, MSME classification, plant and machinery, turnover, NIC code

2. Term loans
   - Equipment, machinery, factory expansion
   - Keywords: term loan, collateral, CGTMSE, SIDBI, machinery finance, repayment capacity

3. Working capital
   - Cash credit, overdraft, stock statements
   - Keywords: cash credit, drawing power, inventory cycle, receivables, stock audit

4. Invoice finance and TReDS
   - Receivable discounting from large buyers
   - Keywords: RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart, buyer acceptance, invoice discounting, annualized cost

5. Supply chain finance
   - Buyer-led bank programs
   - Keywords: anchor buyer, vendor finance, SCF, FMCG buyer, corporate acceptance

6. CGTMSE
   - Collateral-free guarantee-backed credit
   - Keywords: guarantee cover, borrower limit, guarantee fee, collateral-free, MSE loan

7. Mudra, Stand-Up India, PMEGP
   - Non-tech and small manufacturing/service businesses
   - Keywords: Shishu, Kishore, Tarun, margin money subsidy, promoter contribution, employment generation

8. Delayed payment protection
   - MSMED Act and buyer tax pressure
   - Keywords: 45-day rule, Section 15, Section 16, MSEFC, Section 43B(h), buyer deduction disallowance

9. Government procurement
   - GeM, public procurement policy, quality certification
   - Keywords: GeM, vendor assessment, ZED, BIS, FSSAI, EMD exemption, tender eligibility

10. Restructuring and stress
   - Overdue debt, settlement, NPA risk, cash-flow repair
   - Keywords: restructuring, SMA, NPA, one-time settlement, moratorium, debt service coverage

## Founder Question Patterns

1. Which option should I choose first?
2. What is the hidden risk in this term?
3. How much dilution does this actually cost?
4. Should I raise now or wait?
5. Should I take debt, equity, grant, or acquisition?
6. What documents do I need in the next 30 days?
7. Which scheme fits my stage and which should I avoid?
8. Is my advisor, CA, lawyer, or investor wrong?
9. What is the final decision rule?
10. What changes if one number changes: MRR, runway, valuation, ESOP, interest rate, buyer delay?
11. What is the downside case?
12. What should I negotiate and what should I accept?

## Dataset Quality Rules

1. Every record must be scenario-specific, not a reusable template.
2. Every assistant turn must contain math, Indian context, a committed recommendation, and a concrete execution plan.
3. Include current law/scheme details only after official-source verification.
4. Do not train on live market counts, current startup-recognition counts, or short-lived funding statistics as if they are permanent truth.
5. Avoid generic answers such as "consult a CA" or "it depends"; give decision logic and document checks.
6. Include both finance reasoning and operational consequences.
7. Teach the model to separate stable information from changing data.
